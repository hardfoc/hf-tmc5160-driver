Hardware¶

What to connect between your MCU and a TMC5160, with which bus settings and supplies, and the board-level traps to avoid.

All values here come from the TMC5160 / TMC5160A datasheet (Rev. 1.18, 2023-03-01). Pin numbers are given for the eTQFP48 package, with the QFN56 number in brackets. The TMC5130 has the same digital interface; check its own datasheet for its pinout and supply range (TMC5130).

Mode straps¶

SPI_MODE and SD_MODE select the interface and the motion source. Tie them to GND or VCC_IO. Treat them as straps: hard-wire them, or change them only while the chip is unpowered.

SPI_MODE (22) SD_MODE (21) Mode Use with this driver
high low SPI, internal ramp generator Yes: the full API
high high SPI, external STEP/DIR Yes: configuration and diagnostics; motion calls return INVALID_STATE
low low Single-wire UART, internal ramp generator Yes: the full API over UartCommInterface
low high Standalone STEP/DIR, configured by the CFG pins No serial interface

SPI_MODE has an internal pull-down: left open it selects UART or standalone mode, not SPI. The driver reads SD_MODE back from IOIN and refuses ramp, homing, tuning and encoder calls when the chip runs from STEP/DIR.

SPI pins¶

Signal TMC5160 pin Direction (chip) Notes
CSN_CFG3 13 (14) in Active low; hold it low for the whole datagram
SCK_CFG2 14 (15) in
SDI_CFG1 15 (16) in MOSI
SDO_CFG0 16 (17) out, tristate MISO; released while CSN is high, so the bus can be shared

UART pins¶

Signal TMC5160 pin Direction (chip) Notes
DIAG1_SWP 27 (28) in / out Single-wire data. Join the MCU's TX and RX to it
DIAG0_SWN 26 (27) in / out Inverted data for a differential (RS485-like) bus. In a 3.3 V single-wire design tie it to half the I/O voltage; with 5 V I/O it can stay open
SDI_CFG1 (NAI) 15 (16) in Next-address input for sequential addressing (Multi-chip)
SDO_CFG0 (NAO) 16 (17) out Next-address output to the following chip's NAI (high after reset)

Control and status pins¶

The driver addresses these by name (tmc51x0::TMC51x0CtrlPin) and by state (GpioSignal::ACTIVE / INACTIVE); your port turns a state into a level (Porting).

Pin TMC5160 pin What it does Driver use
DRV_ENN (in) 28 (29) High switches the power stage off (outputs floating) motorControl.Enable() / Disable() drive EN
CLK (in) 12 (13) GND for the internal clock, or an external clock SetClkFreq() on your port (optional)
REFL_STEP, REFR_DIR (in) 17 (18), 18 (19) Left / right reference switch, or STEP / DIR when SD_MODE is high switches, homing
ENCA, ENCB, ENCN (in) 24, 23, 25 (24, 23, 26) ABN encoder (SD_MODE low), or DcStep pins encoder
DIAG0, DIAG1 (out) 26 (27), 27 (28) Interrupt / stall / position-compare outputs in SPI mode Optional: read through GpioRead()

ENCA and ENCB have internal pull-downs. With SD_MODE low, pull ENCN to GND or VCC_IO when no encoder is fitted. In open-drain mode the DIAG outputs need an external pull-up of 47 kΩ or less.

Bus settings¶

Setting SPI UART
Mode / format Mode 3 (CPOL = 1, CPHA = 1), MSB first 8N1, single wire, half duplex
Speed Up to 4 MHz on the internal clock; up to 8 MHz with a 16 MHz external clock 9000 baud minimum, up to fCLK/16 (750 kbaud at 12 MHz); the chip detects the baud rate from each frame
Frame 40 bits: address byte (bit 7 = write) and 32 data bits Write: 8 bytes. Read: 4-byte request, 8-byte reply. Sync nibble and CRC8
Chip select Low for the whole datagram; high time tCSH > 2 tCLK + 10 ns n/a
Reply The data of a read arrives with the next datagram After SENDDELAY bit times (default 8; at least 2 × 8 with several nodes)
Timing filter SPI inputs and DRV_ENN ignore pulses shorter than 20 ns A pause of more than 63 bit times resets the frame

The datasheet limits SCK to half the system clock and asks for a further 10 % margin on the internal oscillator, which gives the 4 MHz above. The ESP32 examples run SPI at 500 kHz.

sequenceDiagram
    participant H as Host
    participant C as TMC5160
    H->>C: 1: read XACTUAL
    C-->>H: status + old data (discarded)
    H->>C: 2: read XACTUAL
    C-->>H: status + XACTUAL
    H->>C: 3: write VMAX
    C-->>H: status + XACTUAL
    H->>C: 4: read VMAX
    C-->>H: status + VMAX written in 3

Every reply starts with SPI_STATUS: reset, driver error, StallGuard, standstill, velocity and position reached, and the two stop switches. Communication protocols covers the datagrams in detail.

Power¶

Supply Range Notes
VS (motor bridges, charge pump) 10 V to 55 V Full specification. Down to 8 V with short-to-GND protection inactive. 60 V absolute maximum, including ringing
VSA (5 V and 11.5 V regulators) 10 V to 50 V Normally tied to VS
VCC_IO (digital I/O) 3.0 V to 5.25 V Your MCU's I/O voltage; sets the level of every digital pin
VCC (digital core) 5 V From the chip's own 5VOUT through 2.2 Ω to 3.3 Ω

Connect the MCU ground to the TMC5160 ground plane. Supply current with the driver disabled is 18 mA typical at 24 V and 12 MHz. The internal clock is factory-calibrated to 12 MHz ± 0.5 MHz at 50 °C; an external clock may run from 4 MHz to 18 MHz (10 MHz to 16 MHz recommended).

Size the supply path for the motor, not the chip

The bridge switches the full coil current from VS. Put low-ESR bulk capacitance close to the MOSFETs, keep the sense-resistor ground loop short, and keep the motor connected while VS is on: unplugging a running motor produces a voltage spike that can exceed the 60 V rating.

Sense resistor and current range¶

Two sense resistors set the coil current range. The full-scale RMS current is

IRMS,max = VFS / RSENSE / √2, with VFS = 325 mV typical,

so 50 mΩ gives about 4.6 A RMS and 33 mΩ about 7.0 A RMS (derived from the formula, not measured). The driver scales the current down from there with GLOBAL_SCALER (32 to 256) and IRUN / IHOLD (0 to 31); give it sense_resistor_mohm and it does the arithmetic (Current and chopper).
